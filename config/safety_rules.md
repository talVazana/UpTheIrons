# Workshop Safety & Hazard Rules

## Philosophy
Safety is not an annoying disclaimer; it is an inseparable element of craftsman competence. Every guide, project, and tool tutorial must integrate safety seamlessly.

## Mandatory Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
1. **Eye Protection:** ANSI Z87.1 approved safety glasses with side shields worn at all times near anvil, forge, or grinder.
2. **Hearing Protection:** High-decibel hearing protection (earmuffs or plugs) mandatory for power hammers, grinders, and heavy anvil strikes.
3. **Respiratory Protection:** N95/P100 respirators mandatory when dry grinding steel, handling ceramic fiber forge blanket, or grinding wood/epoxy handle materials.
4. **Clothing & Footwear:** 100% natural fibers (cotton, leather, wool). Synthetic garments (nylon, polyester) melt to skin and are strictly prohibited. Steel-toe leather work boots required.

## Critical Workshop Hazards
1. **Zinc Fume Fever (Galvanized Steel):** Forging galvanized (zinc-coated) hardware releases deadly toxic zinc oxide gas. Muriatic acid soaking or complete chemical stripping required before heating.
2. **Ceramic Fiber Blanket Inhalation:** Refractory ceramic blanket (Kaowool) fibers cause permanent lung scarring (silicosis). All exposed fiber must be rigidized and coated with refractory cement (e.g. Satanite).
3. **Flammable Quench Media:** Quenching red-hot blades in oil creates fireball risk. Metal cover lid must be kept at arm's reach to smother flare-ups. Never use water to extinguish oil fire; Class B fire extinguisher mandatory.
4. **Carbon Monoxide & Ventilation:** Propane and solid fuel forges produce toxic CO. Forced ventilation and dual CO detectors mandatory in enclosed workshops.
