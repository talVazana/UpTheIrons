# Blacksmith Knight — Master Rule Codex

## Overview
This master rules file and its companion documents govern content inclusion, quality standards, safety verifications, metallurgical integrity, product evaluations, and editorial voice for the Blacksmith Knight system.

## Rule Structure
The rules are partitioned into modular domain specifications:
- `mission.md` — Forge philosophy, non-commercial ethos, audience
- `content_rules.md` — Inclusion criteria, priority topics, relevance scoring, rejection boundaries
- `safety_rules.md` — Mandatory safety standards, PPE, ventilation, hazard warnings
- `source_rules.md` — Source quality criteria, anti-crawling boundaries, provenance requirements
- `product_rules.md` — Transparent gear evaluation, pricing integrity, anti-bias policy
- `metallurgy_rules.md` — Strict chemical & thermal accuracy, anti-hallucination mandate
- `editorial_style.md` — Voice, tone, readability, anti-hype principles

## Enforcement
1. **Deterministic Filter:** Evaluated during ingestion pipelines (YouTube, RSS, Product Catalogs).
2. **AI System Instruction:** Guardrails injected into all AI enrichment prompts.
3. **Editorial Overrides:** Human-in-the-loop manual review states (pinned, verified, needs_review, hidden).
