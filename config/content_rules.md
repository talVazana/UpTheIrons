# Content Inclusion & Quality Rules

## Priority Craft Topics
Content must directly address one or more of the following craft domains:
- **Blacksmithing:** Fire maintenance, hammer control, tapering, upsetting, hot punching, drifting, forge welding.
- **Bladesmithing:** Profile shaping, distal taper, bevel grinding, edge geometry, handles and tang construction.
- **Heat Treatment:** Normalizing, annealing, hardening, oil quenching, temper cycles, grain refinement.
- **Metallurgy:** Carbon steels (1084, 1095), alloy steels (5160, 4140, O1, W1), spark testing, alloy identification.
- **Tools & Equipment:** Anvil dressing, hammer re-hanging, tong forging, propane and coal forge construction, 2x72 grinders.
- **Workshop Projects:** Beginner progressive builds (S-hooks, punches, chisels, tongs, camp knives).

## Relevance Thresholds
- High Relevance (>= 0.85): Direct practical forge instruction, detailed metallurgy, tool making.
- Moderate Relevance (0.65 - 0.84): General metalworking, historical blacksmithing, shop setup tips.
- Low Relevance (< 0.65): Reject or route to `needs_review`.

## Mandatory Rejections
Content MUST be rejected or flagged if it contains:
1. **Commercial Spam:** Pure affiliate listicles, drop-shipping ads, sponsored video without educational substance.
2. **Clickbait & Hype:** Sensationalized titles ("You Won't Believe What Happened in the Forge!"), exaggerated claims.
3. **Off-Topic:** General machining, woodworking without forge context, construction unrelated to shop layout.
4. **Dangerous Practices Without Warnings:** Unvented coal forges, water quenching unknown alloys, forging galvanized steel without toxicity warning.
