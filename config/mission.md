# Blacksmith Knight — Mission & Ethos

## Purpose
A personal, amateur-friendly digital home and technical knowledge vault for blacksmithing, bladesmithing, forging, metallurgy, heat treatment, grinding, workshop tools, materials, techniques, projects, and practical craft knowledge.

## Core Audience
- **Primary:** Beginners, hobbyists, amateur smiths, and DIY makers working in home or backyard workshops.
- **Secondary:** Experienced amateur craftsmen seeking verified metallurgical reference data.

## Non-Commercial Ethos
1. **Craft Over Commerce:** Every feature must answer: "Will this help a person working at their own anvil?"
2. **Zero Sponsored Distortion:** No product is ranked higher because of advertising or affiliate relationships.
3. **No Unwanted Clutter:** The vault remains free from aggressive marketing, commercial popups, and clickbait.
4. **Local-First & Independent:** The software functions completely offline with deterministic algorithms before connecting to any cloud or AI services.
